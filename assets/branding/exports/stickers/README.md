# ShinyHub light-mode sticker exports

These are print-ready exports of the approved light-mode Orbit Hub identity.
Both PNGs retain true transparency, the corrected continuous orbit geometry,
and 300 PPI density metadata without resampling. Detached source antialias flecks
are excluded from these production copies so they cannot become stray cut islands;
the approved screen masters remain unchanged.

## Recommended launch sticker: complete logo

`shinyhub-orbit-hub-light-lockup-300ppi.png`

- Full horizontal logo: `2172 × 724 px`
- Includes the complete, exact `ShinyHub` wordmark and emblem
- Printed artwork bounds: `1951 × 412 px`
- Best use: order a 4-inch-wide laptop, case, or equipment sticker
- Effective resolution at that size: approximately `513 PPI`
- Expected finished size with the standard border: approximately `4.00 × 1.00 in`
- Upload instruction: retain Sticker Mule's standard 0.1-inch white border

Use this version while the identity is still being introduced: the symbol and
name travel together, so every sticker teaches the mark instead of assuming it
is already recognized.

## Secondary sticker: emblem only

`shinyhub-orbit-hub-light-mark-300ppi.png`

- Emblem-only artwork: `701 × 587 px`
- Printed artwork at 300 PPI: `1.79 × 1.37 in`
- Expected size with the standard border: approximately `1.99 × 1.57 in`
- Reserve for later use once the Orbit Hub symbol has recognition of its own
- Upload instruction: retain Sticker Mule's standard 0.1-inch white border

Sticker vendors may scale either file down without quality loss. Review the
vendor's digital proof to confirm the white border and cut line before ordering.

Suggested proof note: “White-vinyl die-cut sticker. Add one continuous 0.10-inch
white border around the outer artwork silhouette. No internal cuts. Preserve
proportions and colors. Transparent internal regions should remain white
substrate. Size by the final cutline. Send proof for approval.”
