# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

ShinyHub serves data and analytics teams that need R, Python, and other interactive dashboards to perform reliably in production. The buyer is typically a Head of Data, Analytics, or Engineering; the product champion is an R/Python developer or analytics engineer; and a platform or DevOps engineer operates the service. Dashboard viewers are the ultimate beneficiaries: their experience should remain fast and dependable.

## Product Purpose

ShinyHub exists to deliver performant dashboard experiences. It gives teams a self-hosted or managed platform for deploying and operating interactive applications, with the authentication, scaling, and operational capabilities needed for production use. Success means viewers receive fast, reliable dashboards while the teams behind them avoid unnecessary operational complexity.

## Positioning

ShinyHub is the self-hosted or managed platform for deploying fast, reliable R, Python, and other interactive dashboards—with built-in authentication, scaling, and operations.

## Brand Personality

Fast, dependable, and focused. ShinyHub should feel performance-led and operationally trustworthy, with language and interactions free of unnecessary complexity.

## Anti-references

ShinyHub should not feel like a complex hyperscale cloud console, a fragile developer demo tool, or a visually noisy and flashy SaaS dashboard.

## Design Principles

1. **Make dashboard performance the outcome.** Frame infrastructure, scaling, and operations by how they improve the viewer's experience.
2. **Earn operational trust.** Make system state, consequences, progress, and recovery paths clear wherever operators act.
3. **Keep expertise focused.** Support powerful production workflows without importing the complexity of a hyperscale cloud console.
4. **Serve the full delivery chain.** Help buyers see business value, developers ship confidently, operators maintain reliability, and viewers reach dashboards quickly.
5. **Prefer durable clarity.** Use familiar interaction patterns and restrained communication instead of novelty, fragility, or visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, clearly visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, and sufficient contrast in both dark and light themes.
